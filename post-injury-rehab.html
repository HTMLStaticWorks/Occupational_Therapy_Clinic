Clinical Conditions

Post-Injury Obstacles We Overcome Together

We target both primary physical limitations and secondary compensatory movement habits.

Post-Surgical Joint Stiffness

Breaking down scar tissue contractures and restoring smooth joint articulation following orthopaedic procedures.

Muscle Atrophy & Weakness

Targeted bio-resistive strengthening to rebuild muscle mass lost during long casting or bed rest periods.

Neuromuscular Re-Education

Retraining nerve firing patterns to eliminate awkward limp compensations and restore normal gait & reach.

Structured Protocol

Our 4-Phase Post-Injury Protocols

We work in direct consultation with your orthopedic surgeon to ensure your movement loading strictly adheres to healing timelines.

  • Phase I (Protection & Swelling Reduction): Cryotherapy, gentle lymphatic drainage, and passive range of motion.
  • Phase II (Active Mobilization): Active-assisted movements and scar tissue friction mobilization.
  • Phase III (Progressive Strength): Resistance band work, proprioception balance drills, and functional lifting.
  • Phase IV (Work & Sport Return): Task-specific simulation testing to ensure zero risk of re-injury.
