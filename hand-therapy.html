Clinical Scope

Hand & Wrist Conditions We Treat

Our Certified Hand Therapists (CHTs) manage complex post-surgical and non-surgical upper extremity conditions.

Wrist & Finger Fractures

Post-cast immobilization recovery, joint stiffness reduction, and progressive range-of-motion exercises.

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Median nerve gliding, ergonomic splinting, and soft tissue release techniques for chronic numbness.

Tendon & Nerve Repairs

Post-operative tendon repair protocols, scar management, and active motion guarding.

Rheumatoid & Osteoarthritis

Joint protection education, thermal therapy, and custom functional thumb spica splints.

Repetitive Strain Injuries

De Quervain’s tenosynovitis, trigger finger release, and workstation ergonomic modifications.

Dupuytren’s Contracture

Post-fasciectomy extension splinting, tissue softening massage, and grip expansion routines.

Advanced Modalities

State-of-the-Art Hand Rehabilitation Techniques

We utilize precision therapeutic tools and custom-fabricated orthotics crafted directly in our clinic clinic lab.

  • Custom Thermoplastic Splinting: Custom molded dynamic and static orthoses fabricated during your appointment for immediate joint stabilization.
  • Ultrasound & Fluidotherapy: Thermal modalities that increase tissue elasticity and decrease joint pain prior to active stretching.
  • Sensory Desensitization & Re-education: Rebuilding tactile nerve pathways after nerve lacerations or amputations.
Patient Progress

Phased Fine Motor Progression

How we rebuild your hand strength from early post-op protection to full functional power.

Phase 1

Protection & Pain Reduction

Edema management through gentle compression wraps, custom splint fitting, and passive ROM exercises.

Phase 2

Active Motion & Pinch Control

Introducing therapeutic putty, pegboard coordination drills, and active-assisted tendon gliding.

Phase 3

Functional Strengthening

Simulating real-world tasks like turning keys, opening jars, typing, and gripping tools pain-free.
