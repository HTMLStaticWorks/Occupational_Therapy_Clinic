Understanding Needs

Sensory Processing Challenges We Support

Every child responds uniquely to sensory stimuli. We tailor therapy for both hypersensitive and hyposensitive profiles.

Auditory & Tactile Sensitivity

Hypersensitivity to loud environments, clothing tags, grooming tasks, or specific food textures.

Vestibular & Balance Instability

Difficulty maintaining balance, gravitational insecurity, fear of swinging, or frequent clumsiness.

Fine & Gross Motor Praxix

Struggles with shoe tying, buttoning, pencil grip, scissors control, or riding a bicycle.

Self-Regulation & Focus

Difficulty transitioning between activities, overstimulation meltdowns, and emotional coping skills.

Clinical Playground

Our Specialized Sensory Integration Gym

Designed to feel like an inviting indoor adventure zone, our sensory gym provides controlled multi-sensory feedback in a safe, fun setting.

  • Suspended Equipment: Bolster swings, hammock swings, and platform swings to strengthen vestibular pathways.
  • Proprioceptive Tactile Zones: Weighted blankets, crash pads, climbing nets, and deep-pressure squeeze rollers.
  • Calming Snoezelen Rooms: Fiber optic visual lighting and calming auditory therapy for self-soothing.
Play With Purpose

Evidence-Based Sensory Routines

Therapeutic games designed to look like play while strengthening brain pathways.

Tactile Exploration Bins

Gradual exposure to therapy putty, kinetic sand, water beads, and foam to reduce tactile defensiveness.

Heavy Work & Muscle Input

Carrying weighted balls, pushing foam obstacle blocks, and wall climbs to ground body awareness.

Bilateral Motor Coordination

Two-handed stringing tasks, balance beam navigation, and catching beanbags while swinging.
