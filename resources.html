Practical Advice

Ergonomic Modifications for Everyday Freedom

Simple, high-impact changes to your daily environment can significantly reduce joint strain and fatigue:

  • Lever Handles Over Doorknobs: Replace round door handles with lever handles to eliminate painful wrist twisting.
  • Built-Up Utensil Handles: Use foam tubing on forks, spoons, and pens to lessen pinch force required.
  • Joint Conservation Energy Pacing: Alternate heavy tasks with resting periods to prevent inflammation spikes.
First Visit Prep

How to Prepare for Your Initial Evaluation

Follow these 3 simple steps to ensure a smooth, comprehensive first visit.

01

Bring Surgical Notes

Bring physician referrals, surgical operative reports, or X-ray imaging reports if available.

02

Wear Comfortable Attire

Wear loose-fitting clothing that allows easy exposure of the shoulder, elbow, wrist, or knee being evaluated.

03

List Daily Goals

Jot down 2-3 specific activities (e.g. typing 30 mins, opening jar) you wish to perform without pain.
