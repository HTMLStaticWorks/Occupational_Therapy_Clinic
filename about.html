Our Identity & Story

Restoring Function, Inspiring Hope

Founded on the principle that purposeful movement transforms lives, RehabNest provides evidence-based, compassionate occupational therapy tailored to individual goals.

Core Purpose

Empowering People of All Ages to Live Life to the Fullest

Our mission is to bridge the gap between physical limitation and daily freedom. We don't just treat symptoms or isolated muscles; we focus on how you interact with your environment, family, work, and community.

Every treatment plan at RehabNest is co-created with the patient, ensuring respect, dignity, and real-world relevance every step of the way.

Clinical Principles

The Four Pillars of RehabNest Care

How our clinical methodology guarantees high-quality, long-term functional recovery.

Evidence-Based Practice

Utilizing research-backed sensory retraining, biofeedback, and neuromuscular re-education modalities.

Environmental Adaptation

Evaluating home and workplace barriers to recommend practical ergonomics and adaptive assistive technology.

Interdisciplinary Synergy

Collaborating with your primary care physicians, surgeons, physical therapists, and family members.

Empathetic Support

Building positive psychological resilience so you stay motivated through every milestone of healing.

Our Spaces

Designed for Comfort, Safety, & Specialized Training

Our 8,000 sq ft clinic features dedicated therapy suites tailored for distinct rehabilitation needs:

  • Custom Hand Therapy Lab: Outfitted with high-precision splint fabrication tools, ultrasound, and fine motor testing rigs.
  • Pediatric Sensory gym: Featuring suspended swings, climbing walls, tactile ball pits, and calming soundscapes.
  • Daily Living Apartment Suite: A simulated kitchen, bedroom, and bathroom for practical daily skill retraining.
